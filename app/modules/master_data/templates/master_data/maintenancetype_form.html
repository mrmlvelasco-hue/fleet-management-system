    Configurable via System Administration → Lookups (MAINTENANCE_CATEGORY). Preventive orders require a completed checklist before closing.
    KM/Calendar intervals are configured per vehicle Make & Model in PM Templates, not here — a single Maintenance Type (e.g. "5,000 KM PMS") can have a different interval for a Honda City vs. a Toyota Hilux.

    • Category Rules: Preventive maintenance types automatically enforce checklist completions prior to order closure.
    • Interval Assignment: Specific mileage or time triggers are managed inside individual PM Schedule Templates.
